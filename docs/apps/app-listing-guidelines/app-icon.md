# App icon guidelines

Learn how to design the perfect icon for your app.

Copy page

The app icon is the visual representation of your app in the Apps Marketplace. Designing an app icon is all about balance. You want it to be both unique and recognizable, while also adapting to different shapes and levels of detail. Maintaining visual consistency is key to a strong user experience.

  * **Keep it simple:** When creating icons for your app, simplicity is key. Use a unique concept or element to represent your app and avoid adding too many details. Keep the background simple to put emphasis on the primary image, and avoid overfilling the icon with content.
  * **Use graphic images instead of photos for icons:** Create graphics to highlight key features. Avoid using photos that may lose detail when viewed at a small size.
  * **Design it in a full-bleed square image:** Use the 1:1 ratio, with a specific size of 512px x 512px. Using this format will ensure that your app icon will be consistently sized, if used in different parts of the platform.
  * **Avoid adding text to your icon:** Minimize the use of text unless essential for your brand or user experience.



Only use content that you have the rights to use. For example, don't use celebrity images or names.

## Using a platform's logo

If your app connects Canva with another platform or product, you can use the other platform's logo as your app icon. For example, a Google Drive app might use the Google Drive logo.

  * **Get permission:** Make sure the platform has approved your use of its logo before you submit your listing.
  * **Use the platform's official logo mark:** Use the symbol, not the wordmark. For example, the Mailchimp Freddie mark, or the OpenAI mark.
  * **Use the mark that matches your app's name:** If your app is named after a product, use that product's mark. If it's named after the parent brand, use the parent brand's mark.
  * **Follow the platform's brand guidelines:** Use the logo exactly as the platform publishes it. Don't recolor, redraw, outline, or crop it.



## Do

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon1.dbdb4aaa.png)

**DO** incorporate recognizable brand elements, like logos or specific colors, to leverage strong brand associations.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon2.ba982a2a.png)

**DO** use graphics reflective of the app’s output.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon3.534f99d8.png)

**DO** opt for a split-screen layout if showcasing before/after states helps convey the app's function.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon4.a6f9aec6.png)

**DO** exaggerate output details to aid recognition of the app's function at a glance.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon5.593b1347.png)

**DO** consider the legibility of line-based graphics at a smaller scale.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon6.d411a9f8.png)

**DO** prioritize clean lines and forms.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon7.749fe3da.png)

**DO** choose a background color that complements the foreground elements.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon8.5f82d5d8.png)

**DO** pair highly visual or intricate backgrounds with simple foregrounds or logos.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-do-icon9.1ca7578c.png)

**DO** size and center the mark on the keyline grid, so it aligns with other app icons in the Apps Marketplace.

## Don't

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon1.91e47deb.png)

**DON'T** overcrowd the app icon with unnecessary details, complicating the design.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon2.58174b9d.png)

**DON'T** use low-contrast colors or thin lines, risking legibility at a small scale.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon3.09711769.png)

**DON'T** add a border radius to the background, as it may result in a double-border effect when a border gets applied in Canva.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon4.003386f8.png)

**DON'T** use cultural references that may not be universally understood. This helps ensure inclusivity and helps prevent misinterpretations.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon5.617498c5.png)

**DON'T** use transparent backgrounds. This will avoid contrast issues when viewed in light and dark mode.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon6.8f4bff72.png)

**DON'T** make icon elements larger than the keyline guide suggests ([view the app icon keyline template⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/design/DAF5cbL3GRY/RoK7LhDuUIqsIDgw7XxqNg/view?utm_content=DAF5cbL3GRY&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link&utm_source=publishsharelink&mode=preview)).

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon7.9c929205.png)

**DON'T** use photos that don’t convey much about the app and lose detail when scaled down.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon8.286c1bd3.png)

**DON'T** use text unless it’s part of the logo.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon9.3ab43c10.png)

**DON'T** combine the Canva logo and another platform's logo in the icon.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/apps-dont-icon10.753ee5cb.png)

**DON'T** place a platform's mark on a Canva palette color unless the platform's brand guidelines allow it.
