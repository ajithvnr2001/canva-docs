## Apps

For what an app is on each of the Developers SDK surfaces, see [Apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/platform-concepts-apps/).

## Assets

For what an asset is, and how apps interact with assets, see [Assets](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/uploading-assets/).

## Designs

For what a design is, and how apps interact with designs, see [Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs/).

## Folders

For what a folder is, and how apps interact with folders, see [Folders](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/platform-concepts-folders/).

## Teams, groups, and users

For how Canva organizes users into organizations, teams, and groups, see [Teams, groups, and users](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/platform-concepts-teams-groups-and-users/).
