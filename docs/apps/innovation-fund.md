# Monetizing apps

How you can be rewarded for developing apps on Canva that empower our community.

Copy page

At Canva, we've seen that the key to viral adoption is creating apps that are simple to onboard, delightful to use, and frictionless to access. Minimizing barriers like paywalls or logins helps users get straight to the magic. But we also know how much time, energy, and cost goes into building great tools.

That's why we're committed to supporting developers who create standout apps for our global community. Whether your app empowers creativity, simplifies a workflow, or just wows people, there's now more ways to earn with Canva.

To receive payments through Canva's monetization programs, be sure to [set up your payout details](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/payout-details/).

## Ways to monetize your app

### 1\. Premium Apps Program

Earn recurring revenue by offering advanced features through the Premium Apps Program. When your app is part of this program, it can be bundled with Canva paid plans — giving you a payout whenever your app is used.

To learn more and apply, head to the [Premium Apps Program⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/developers/premium-apps-program/). For SDK integration and design guidelines, see the [Premium Apps docs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/premium-apps/).

### 2\. External payment links

Want to offer exclusive features or content outside of Canva? While the Canva Developers SDK doesn't support in-app purchases, you're free to direct users to pay through your own site.

To learn more, see [External payment links](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/accepting-payments/).
