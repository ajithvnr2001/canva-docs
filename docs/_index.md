## Browse by use case

* * *

### Design generation

Programmatically create new designs, or give users tools to help them create and edit designs inside Canva.

[Designs API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/designs/)

Create and interact with designs in a user’s Canva library.

[Autofill API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/autofill-guide/)

Generate a finished design by filling a Brand Template with your own data.

[Design Imports API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/design-imports/)

Turn files like PDFs, PowerPoint, or Photoshop documents into an editable Canva design.

[Generate designs with AI](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/tools/generate-design/)

Let users create designs by chatting with their AI assistant.

[Edit designs with AI](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/tools/start-editing-transaction/)

Let users edit designs by chatting with their AI assistant.

[Design Editor intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/design-editor/)

Build an app inside Canva that lets users create and modify designs as they work in Canva.

[Merges API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/merges/)

Combine multiple designs into one.

See more usecases

* * *

### Content automation

Turn structured data from external systems into on-brand designs at scale.

[Autofill API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/autofill-guide/)

Turn structured data into finished, on-brand designs at scale.

[Brand Templates API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/brand-templates/)

Publish, list, and manage the templates your automation fills.

[Webhooks](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/webhooks/)

Get real-time notifications when comments, approvals, or sharing happen on a design.

[Data Connector intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/data-connector/)

Build an app inside Canva that pulls data from an external source straight into designs.

[Content Publisher intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/content-publisher/)

Build an app that publishes designs to external platforms directly from Canva.

* * *

### Assets

Connect and manage a brand or user’s asset library between Canva and an external system.

[Assets API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/assets/)

Upload assets into a user’s Canva library.

[Folders API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/folders/)

Organize and manage folders in a user’s Canva library.

[Digital Asset Management app template](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/app-templates/digital-asset-management/)

A ready-made pattern for surfacing your DAM’s content inside the Canva editor.

[Upload assets with AI](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/tools/upload-asset-from-url/)

Let an AI assistant upload assets straight into a user’s Canva library.

* * *

### Social media

Get content from Canva out to social platforms.

[Content Publisher intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/content-publisher/implementation-guide/)

Build an app that publishes designs to social platforms directly from Canva.

[Resizes API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/resizes/)

Resize a design to the exact dimensions each social platform needs.

[Exports API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/exports/)

Export a design as an image or video file, ready to post anywhere.

* * *

### Presentations

Build and export decks – from data-driven generation to specialized in-editor slide tools.

[Autofill API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/autofill-guide/)

Generate a full deck from structured data – e.g. auto-build a pitch deck from CRM records.

[Exports API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/exports/)

Export a finished presentation as PDF or PPTX for distribution.

[Design Editor intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/design-editor/)

Build specialized in-editor tools for slide-specific workflows.

[Data Connector intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/data-connector/)

Build an app inside Canva that connects live data – like a sales dashboard or report – to a presentation.

* * *

### Video

Add and edit video and audio content in a design, then export it in the format you need.

[Design Editor intent](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/design-editor/)

Build an app inside Canva that adds audio tracks and video elements to a design.

[Exports API](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/exports/)

Export video designs in the format you need.

[Export designs with AI](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/tools/export-design/)

Let an AI assistant download a finished video design as an MP4.
