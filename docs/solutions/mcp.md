## Frequently asked questions

  * * * *

  * What is Canva MCP?

Canva MCP is a Model Context Protocol server that connects AI agents and assistants to Canva's full visual communication platform.

  * * * *

  * How does Canva MCP work?

Canva MCP uses the open Model Context Protocol standard to expose Canva's design capabilities as tools your AI agent can call. Simply connect, configure, and your agent can start creating. [Read the complete developer documentation⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/) to learn more.

  * * * *

  * Which AI assistants and frameworks are compatible with Canva MCP?

Canva MCP is compatible with any MCP-supported AI assistant or framework, including Claude, ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and more. [Check top AI tools already connected to Canva⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-connectors-canva/).

  * * * *

  * Is Canva MCP enterprise-ready?

Yes, Canva MCP is built on infrastructure trusted by the world's top companies. Its access controls, brand governance, and asset management ensure every workflow stays secure and on-brand.

  * * * *

  * How do I set up Canva MCP?

Setting up Canva MCP requires no custom development. Simply install the MCP server, configure your connection, and authenticate – your AI agent will be ready to design in no time. [Read the full setup guide⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/access/) to know more about access and permissions.

  * * * *

  * What makes Canva MCP different from other AI design tools?

Unlike generic AI image generators, Canva MCP produces fully editable, layered designs with Brand Kits and templates built-in. It helps you combine creativity with productivity, ensuring every output is on-brand, in-context, and ready to refine or ship – wherever and whenever you need it.

  * * * *

  * What design capabilities come with Canva MCP?

Canva MCP supports bulk asset generation powered by Autofill and Brand Kits. It also lets your AI agents query your Canva workspace directly. Your agents can surface past designs, extract messaging themes, and turn content history into actionable context. You can also ask your AI to transform one design into multiple formats for different channels. [See the actions an assistant can take⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/help/mcp-canva-usage/).

  * * * *

  * How does Canva MCP fit into an agentic AI stack?

Canva MCP is designed as infrastructure, not a standalone tool. Its design actions are built to chain together, making it easy to add design as a native step in any agentic pipeline or automated workflow.

  * * * *

  * What should I keep in mind when designing my use case?

Be a good human when integrating Canva MCP into your agent or assistant:

    * Represent Canva accurately – don't imply endorsement or certification.
    * Don't bulk download users' assets or designs.
    * Don't train on users' data.
    * Keep each user on their own account and permissions.
    * Don't hide credit usage or work around rate limits.

Read our [usage policy⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/mcp/usage-policy/) to check our complete guidelines.
