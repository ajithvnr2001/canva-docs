# Announcing our new engineering blog

Canva engineering blog is moving to its new home.

![](https://www.canva.dev/_next/static/media/thumbnail.f9145cc6.png)

* * *

  * 


[Vikram Goyal⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vikram-goyal-a1a43b1/)May 4, 2023

* * *

Almost two years ago, a small team of engineers within Canva decided to give our engineering blog a fresh start to share Canva’s technology insights with the wider engineering world.

Fast forward to today and twenty-five blog posts later, we’d like to announce that we’re moving our engineering blog to [https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/](https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/) with canva.dev becoming the central hub of all engineering activities at Canva. So please check it out and don’t hesitate to give us feedback! Rest assured, we’ll continue to publish insightful articles about the technology powering Canva there.

We’re incredibly grateful to medium.com, as it’s been a great platform for our blog for the past two years. As this will be our last post on Medium, we encourage you to stay connected by subscribing to our new blog at [https://www.canva.dev/blog/subscribe/](https://www.canva.dev/blog/subscribe/). You’ll receive updates each time we publish a new article — no spam, just pure engineering content.

See you at our new home:

[https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/](https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/).
