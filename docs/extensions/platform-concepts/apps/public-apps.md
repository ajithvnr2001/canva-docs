# Public apps

What is a public app? How does it differ from a team app?

Copy page

A _public app_ is an app that can be made available to all of Canva's users. This is opposed to [team apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/), which can only be made available to members of a specific team.

To release a public app, you must [submit it for review](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/public-apps/submit-public-app/). Someone from Canva will review the app and, if it's approved, you can [release the app](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/public-apps/release-public-app/) to the public.

Once a public app is released, users can find the app:

  * In the Canva editor, via the **More** tab, **Edit image** panel, or **Publish** menu.
  * Outside of the editor, via [canva.com/apps⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/apps)



You can't change a public app into a team app (or vice-versa).
