# Release a team app

Copy page

If you're the owner or administrator of a [team⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams), it's your responsibility to review apps submitted by members of the team. Once you complete this review, you can choose to release or [reject](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/reject-team-app/) the app.

To release a team app:

  1. Navigate to [canva.com/teams⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams).
  2. Switch to the **Apps** tab.
  3. Find the app you're looking for.
  4. Click **Release**.
  5. In the dialog that appears, click **Release app**.



When you release a team app, Canva sends an email to the creator of the app and immediately makes the app available to members of the team.

Unlike [public apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/public-apps/), Canva doesn't translate team apps.
