# Reject a team app

Copy page

If you're the owner or administrator of a [team⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams), it's your responsibility to review apps submitted by members of the team. Once you complete this review, you can choose to [release](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/release-team-app/) or reject the app.

To reject a team app:

  1. Navigate to [canva.com/teams⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams).
  2. Switch to the **Apps** tab.
  3. Find the app you're looking for.
  4. Click **Reject**.
  5. In the dialog that appears, provide a reason for the rejection.
  6. Click **Reject app**.



When you reject a team app, Canva sends the feedback to the creator of the app. To implement the feedback, they must create a new version of the app and resubmit it for review.
