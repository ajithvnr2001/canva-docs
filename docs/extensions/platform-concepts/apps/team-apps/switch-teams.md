# Switch teams

Copy page

You can be a member of multiple teams, so it's important to switch to the relevant team before attempting to create, use, or review [team apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/).

To switch teams:

  1. Open the profile switcher.
  2. Under the **Switch team** heading, select a team.



Canva remembers the selected team between visits.

The **Switch team** heading isn't visible if there aren't any teams to switch between.

If you're not a member of a team, visit [canva.com/teams⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](http://canva.com/teams) to create a team.
