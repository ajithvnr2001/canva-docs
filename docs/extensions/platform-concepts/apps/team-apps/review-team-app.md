# Review a team app

Copy page

If you're the owner or administrator of a [team⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams), it's your responsibility to review apps submitted by members of the team.

Like [public apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/public-apps/), [team apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/) must meet the requirements outlined on the **Submission** page, such as [verifying request signatures](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/backend-development/verify-request/). Any further requirements and guidelines are at the discretion of the team and the reviewer.

Canva is not involved in the reviewing of team apps.

To review a team app:

  1. Navigate to [canva.com/teams⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/teams).
  2. Switch to the **Apps** tab.
  3. Find the app you're looking for.
  4. Click **Preview**. This opens the app in the Canva editor.
  5. Verify that the app meets your team's guidelines.
  6. [Release](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/release-team-app/) or [reject](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/reject-team-app/) the app.



If you're not sure what to look for when reviewing an app, take inspiration from Canva's [UX guidelines](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/distribution/ux-guidelines/).
