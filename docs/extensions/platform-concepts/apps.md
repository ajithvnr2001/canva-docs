# Apps

What are Canva apps? Why create them?

Copy page

Apps are plugins that adds features to Canva. They run in the browser and are built with standard web technologies, such as REST APIs and JavaScript.

## Examples of apps

These are some examples of apps:

  * [Bitmoji⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/apps/AADxCLHNr_o-bitmoji) \- Add Bitmoji avatars to your design.
  * [Duotone⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/apps/AADrJD0kfRQ-duotone) \- Apply a two-color palette to your images.
  * [Pinterest⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/apps/pinterest) \- Post designs to your boards on Pinterest.



You can find more examples at [canva.com/apps⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://canva.com/apps).

## What can apps do?

Canva provides a number of _extensions_ that let developers hook into different parts of Canva.

By integrating with these extensions, an app can:

  * [Add third-party content to Canva](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/extensions/content-extensions/).
  * [Add publish destinations to Canva](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/extensions/publish-extensions/).



A single app can support multiple extensions, enabling it to seamlessly integrate with the entire Canva experience.

### Internal vs. external apps

Some apps, such as [Pexels⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://www.canva.com/apps/pexels), have features that aren't covered in the documentation. This is because we've created these apps internally at Canva, which means they have access to internal APIs.

If a feature isn't documented, you can assume that it's not publicly available. Over time though, our goal is to improve parity between internal and external apps.

Have an idea for a feature? [Create a support ticket⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://canva-external.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/2/group/2) and let us know what it is.

## Why create an app?

There are many reasons to create an app:

  * Reach a huge (and active) audience of millions of users.
  * Build incredible tools for an exciting (and fast-growing) platform.
  * Create features to improve your own Canva experience.



### Benefits for teams

If you're using Canva as part of a team, there are even more benefits:

  * Create apps that are only available to members of your team.
  * Create apps that integrate with your team's internal tools.
  * Manage apps created by members of your team.



To learn how to create apps for teams, refer to [Team apps](https://www.canva.dev/docs/extensions/platform-concepts/apps/team-apps/).

## Where can users find apps?

Users can find apps:

  * In the editor, via the **More** tab, **Edit image** panel, or **Publish** menu.
  * Outside of the editor, via [canva.com/apps⁠(opens in a new tab or window)](https://canva.com/apps).



Canva also runs experiments to increase the discoverability of apps by surfacing them at various points in the user experience.
